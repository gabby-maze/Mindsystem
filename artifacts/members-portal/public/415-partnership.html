MAZE Mirror Spread — M: Mindset Mirror
M · Mindset Mirror
Your
Ecosystem.
Before you can show up for her, you have to understand the environment she's actually in.
The pause between your emotion and your reaction — that's the Mirror Moment. Before every game. Every drive home. Every coach conversation.
"She's not watching the scoreboard.
She's watching you."
Three questions. Every game. Every drive home. Every coach conversation. Ask her — don't answer for her. What she says will surprise you.
Write what she says.
Not what you think she'd say.
Start here. Come back at the end of the season. Notice what changed.

A · Analyze Mirror
Your
Audit.
Awareness doesn't happen by accident. It takes the willingness to see your patterns — not what you intend, but what you actually do.
Not the version you imagine. The car ride home. The silence after a tough loss. The comparison you make out loud without knowing it.
"The habits that feel good to you
aren't always the ones serving her."
You're not auditing her — you're auditing yourself. That's the work.

MAZE Mirror Spread — Z: Zero In Mirror
Z · Zero In Mirror
Your
Focus.
Stop watching the game. Start watching her three things. Your job just shifted from general support to precision instrument.
Her three skills are hers. Not the ones you'd pick. Not the ones that look best on a highlight reel. Honor the choice. Don't optimize it.
"You can't acknowledge
what you don't understand."
Study her skills. Understand them at the depth that would surprise her. That's how she knows you've actually seen her.
Write what you see.
Not what you hoped to see.
Start here. Come back at the end of the season. Notice what changed.

MAZE Mirror Spread — E: Execute Mirror
E · Execute Mirror
Your
Actions.
Every gym day, you are choosing one of two roles. Manager speaks for her. Fixes for her. Fills the silence. Collaborator asks. Waits. Witnesses.
One builds dependence. One builds ownership. The season is here. Every game. Every drive home. Every Sunday. Use the system.
"Intentions don't raise athletes.
Actions do."
The process is the point. The outcome is the byproduct. One behavior at a time.
Write what you did.
Not what you meant to do.
Start here. Come back at the end of the season. Notice what changed.
