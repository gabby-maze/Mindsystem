The Curriculum

MindSystem: Built for Blue Oak.

MindSystem is the mental performance curriculum Gabby has built over the last several years working with athletes and families at the club level. For Blue Oak, it becomes the shared framework that ties together everything the volleyball program builds — on the court, in the gym, and at home.

This is the curriculum for the volleyball players and their parents. Not a separate add-on. The backbone of the whole program. Blue Oak's mission is rooted in community, curiosity, joy, kindness, respect, and responsibility. MindSystem gives the volleyball program a concrete system to live those values — structured, trackable, and shared between athlete and parent from the first week.

Learn more about MindSystem and see how it works across Gabby's full program at gabbycole.com. Blue Oak athletes and parents will be working within this system as part of the consulting partnership.

The Family Journal

What Parents Receive.

Every Blue Oak family also receives the Parent Alignment Guide — a physical journal built specifically for the parents in this program. This is not a handout or a pamphlet. It's an education.

The research is clear: programs stall when parents and athletes are operating with different definitions of success. The Parent Alignment Guide gives the parents a shared language, a shared picture of what progress actually looks like, and a shared system for tracking that progress alongside their athlete — week to week, through the whole season.

Shared Language
Parents learn the same MAZE framework the athletes are working through so both sides are speaking the same system.
Shared Definition
A concrete, written picture of what success looks like for their daughter — defined by the athlete, not by the parent.
Shared Tracking
Weekly checkpoints and reflection pages that keep parents actively involved and patient with the process.
The moment a parent has a name for what they're watching, they stop panicking about the parts they can't control. That's what this journal does.
