Before We Talk

How to prepare for our call.

Five minutes of honest reflection before we meet is the difference between a good call and a clear one.

1

Know where she's at right now

Not just her stats or her standing on the team. Where is she mentally? Is she playing with confidence or playing scared? Be honest with yourself about what you're actually seeing right now. Not what you wish you were seeing.

2

Name what you've already tried

Private coaches, club changes, conversations with the coach, books, podcasts. What have you done so far to try to fix this? Come ready to talk about what's worked, what hasn't, and what's still missing.

3

Get honest about your role

This call isn't just about her. It's about the ecosystem around her, and you're part of that ecosystem. Come ready to look at your own part in this, not from a place of guilt, but from a place of readiness to do something about it.
