What's Included

Everything you need
to actually start.

  • 90-Minute Planning Call with Gabby

    One call, split into two halves. The first 45 minutes, Gabby works directly with your athlete. The second 45 minutes, she works with the parent. By the end, your athlete's Compass Journal is built and the parent side is walked through in full.

  • Workbooks + Surveys

    Before the call, both the athlete and the parent receive a workbook. These give you a clear picture of what you will be working through together on the call. After the session, you will be given time to complete those workbooks and submit your responses through a survey. Gabby uses those responses to build out the parent journal. The athlete journal is already printed and shipped. The session is where you fill it in together.

  • 6 Months of Courtside Conversations

    A live monthly call, first Wednesday of every month at 12pm. Bring any question, any situation, anything that comes up during the season. This is your ongoing touchpoint with Gabby for the next six months.

  • MindSystem Training Library

    Access to the instructional video library. The how-to videos for each section of the MAZE framework, the parent alignment guides, and the tools you reference as you work through the system.
