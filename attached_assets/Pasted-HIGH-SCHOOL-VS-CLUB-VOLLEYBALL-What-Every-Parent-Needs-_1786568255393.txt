HIGH SCHOOL VS. CLUB VOLLEYBALL

What Every Parent Needs to Know (That No One Tells You)

PRODUCT DESCRIPTION

You're not crazy. Nobody explained this part.

Your daughter plays club and she plays high school, and somewhere in the middle of both you realized they are not the same sport. The room feels different. The coaching feels different. The expectations feel different. And no one has told you why, so you're left guessing whether the season you're watching is a bad program, a bad fit, or exactly what it was always supposed to be.

That's not a parenting failure. It's what happens when two systems share a name and nobody says out loud that they have two different jobs.

This guide gives you the comparison. Not motivation, not platitudes, and not a list of drills. Fifteen sections on how high school volleyball and club volleyball actually differ, built from surveys of more than 300 parents, athletes, and coaches, and written by someone who has sat in every seat at the table: former Division I player, daughter of a 40 year coach, coach to more than 3,000 athletes, and mom to twin 14 year olds playing right now.

Every quote inside is real. None of them are mine.

Who it's for

Parents with a daughter in both systems, or about to be. Especially anyone who has walked out of a high school gym confused, watched a tryout that didn't feel right, or wondered whether the club season is worth what it costs.

What it is not

This is a comparison guide, not a training program. It gives you the map of both systems and your role inside them. It will not tell you what to say in every conversation, and it isn't a substitute for the deeper systems work.
