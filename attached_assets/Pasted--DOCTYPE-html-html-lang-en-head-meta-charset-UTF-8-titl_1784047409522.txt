  <h1>Summer Training Checklist:<br>Building <span class="accent">Both Games.</span></h1>
  <p class="sub">Balance the physical work everyone can see with the intangible work that actually separates in-season performance.</p>

  <!-- PHYSICAL GAME -->
  <div class="section-header">
    <div class="badge pink">P</div>
    <div class="section-title">The Physical Game
      <span class="tag">WHAT YOU'RE PROBABLY ALREADY DOING</span>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="item"><div class="box"></div><div class="item-text">Private lessons or skill-specific reps</div></div>
  <div class="item"><div class="box"></div><div class="item-text">College camps / exposure camps</div></div>
  <div class="item"><div class="box"></div><div class="item-text">Strength &amp; conditioning</div></div>
  <div class="item"><div class="box"></div><div class="item-text">Ball control / touches outside of team practice</div></div>
  <div class="item"><div class="box"></div><div class="item-text">Active recovery days (movement, not shutdown)</div></div>

  <hr class="divider">

  <!-- INTANGIBLE GAME -->
  <div class="section-header">
    <div class="badge teal">I</div>
    <div class="section-title">The Intangible Game
      <span class="tag">WHAT'S USUALLY MISSING</span>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="item"><div class="box"></div><div class="item-text">Practice <strong>composure under pressure</strong> in a low-stakes setting (a camp, a pickup game — not a tryout)</div></div>
  <div class="item"><div class="box"></div><div class="item-text">Notice her <strong>self-talk after a mistake</strong> — name it, don't fix it yet</div></div>
  <div class="item"><div class="box"></div><div class="item-text">Role-play <strong>receiving hard coach feedback</strong> without getting defensive</div></div>
  <div class="item"><div class="box"></div><div class="item-text">Build a <strong>between-points reset routine</strong> now, while nothing's on the line</div></div>
  <div class="item"><div class="box"></div><div class="item-text">Have one real conversation about <strong>what she wants</strong> from next season — not what she thinks she should want</div></div>
  <div class="item"><div class="box"></div><div class="item-text">Identify <strong>one recurring in-game reaction pattern</strong> from last season worth changing</div></div>
  <div class="item"><div class="box"></div><div class="item-text">Practice sitting with <strong>disappointment</strong> (a bad rep, a lost point) without spiraling</div></div>
  <div class="item"><div class="box"></div><div class="item-text">Write down <strong>her season goals</strong> in her own words, before anyone else's expectations get added</div></div>

  <hr class="divider">

  <!-- CONVERGENCE CHECK -->
  <div class="convergence">
    <div class="section-title" style="font-size:22px;">The Convergence Check</div>
    <div class="item" style="background:transparent;padding:10px 0;"><div class="box"></div><div class="item-text">By the time the season starts, can she name her go-to reset routine without thinking?</div></div>
    <div class="item" style="background:transparent;padding:10px 0;"><div class="box"></div><div class="item-text">Does she have language for what she's feeling instead of just reacting to it?</div></div>
    <div class="item" style="background:transparent;padding:10px 0;"><div class="box"></div><div class="item-text">Has she practiced the hard conversation with a coach before she's actually in it?</div></div>
    <p style="font-size:14px;color:var(--lgray);margin:18px 0 0;line-height:1.6;">If you can check those three at the top of the season, the physical work and the inner work converge — <strong style="color:var(--white);">that's when she takes off.</strong></p>
